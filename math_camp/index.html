Questions

Frequently asked questions

Registration is a flat $135 for every camper, whether you attend in person or online — and discount codes (like the 10% one hidden in the crane on our About Us page) can lower it. The fee is paid by Interac e-Transfer once you complete the registration form, and it helps cover materials, facilities, and refreshments across the 10 days.
This is an accelerated program covering the full MTH1W curriculum in 10 days. It's best suited for students who already have strong math fundamentals and are ready to be challenged. If you're one of the top math students in your Grade 8 class or early Grade 9, this camp is built for you.
All 10 days run at Sheridan College — Trafalgar Campus in Oakville. Click the location to get directions in Google Maps. Specific room assignments and drop-off / pick-up details will be emailed to registered families ahead of Day 1.
No — the program was built around the HDSB MTH1W curriculum, so HDSB students are our primary audience, but we warmly welcome strong Grade 9 math students from any school board.
Sessions are taught by top Grade 11 & 12 HDSB students who have already completed and excelled at this material, with a faculty advisor present for support and safety. Campers who fall behind also get free one-on-one help from Grade 9 & 10 peer tutors.
Bring a device (laptop, tablet, or Chromebook), your own lunch, a water bottle, and a curious mind. All math materials and problem sets are provided. Lunch breaks are a fun social time, so pack something you'll enjoy.
We have 40 spots open for Summer Camp 2026. Register early — once all 100 are filled, additional sign-ups go to a waitlist and we'll reach out if a spot opens up.
We strongly encourage attending both weeks since the material builds across the two weeks. However, we understand scheduling conflicts — please note your availability in the registration form and we'll do our best to accommodate you.
